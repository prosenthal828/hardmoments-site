Privacy Policy

Effective September 15, 2026 · Updated November 15, 2026

The short version: HardMoments doesn't collect your data. Everything you enter stays on your phone, including your account. The one exception is voice dictation: on some phones, Google's or Apple's speech service turns your voice into text. There are no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and we never sell or share anything, because we never receive it.

What the app stores on your phone

To work, HardMoments saves what you type or choose, in the app's private storage on your device:

  • Each child's first name or nickname, and their birthday if you add it
  • The temperament sliders and the moments you chose to focus on
  • The first names of the adults on your family team
  • Each adult's private answers and the plans you sign together
  • Your "How did it go?" logs, special-time and praise logs
  • Your pediatrician's and nurse line's phone numbers, if you add them
  • Photos you add to the photo log, and any note you attach to them

None of this is sent to us or to anyone else. We can't see it, and we can't recover it for you.

Your account

The Safety tab works with no account at all. To use Right now, Playbook and Connect, you create an account with your name, email address and a password. That account is stored on your phone like everything else: the password is saved only as a secure hash, never as plain text, and none of it is sent to us. Because the account lives on your device, it doesn't carry over to another phone, and we can't reset your password for you.

Payments

HardMoments is free. There are no in-app purchases or subscriptions, and the app never asks for payment details.

Permissions

  • Camera. Used only when you tap "Take a photo" in the photo log. Photos are compressed on the phone and saved in the app's private storage. Nothing is uploaded, and the app never tries to identify what's in a photo.
  • Microphone. Used only when you tap "Dictate the note" in the photo log. Your phone's speech recognition turns your voice into text. HardMoments prefers on-device recognition, but on some phones Google's or Apple's speech service may process the audio. HardMoments doesn't save recordings.
  • Photos. "Choose from your photos" uses Android's photo picker, so the app only sees the one photo you pick.
  • Phone calls. When you tap a number, HardMoments hands it to your phone app. It doesn't place calls itself or read your call history.
  • Internet. Used to open the sources we cite (such as the AAP, CDC and NHS) in an in-app browser, and to check for and download app updates. Those services have their own privacy policies.

Children

HardMoments is for adults aged 18 and older. It isn't directed at children, and we don't knowingly collect information from children. Information you enter about your children stays on your phone.

App stores

Google Play and the Apple App Store may collect information about downloads, purchases and crashes under their own privacy policies. We don't receive personal information from them about you.

Keeping it safe and deleting it

Your information is protected by your phone's own security, so we recommend using a screen lock. You can delete individual photos in the app. Uninstalling HardMoments, or clearing its storage in your phone's settings, permanently deletes everything it saved, including your account.

Not medical advice

HardMoments isn't a medical service and doesn't diagnose anything. Its safety checklists are based on published guidance to help you decide when to call your doctor. In an emergency, call 911.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top. If HardMoments ever starts sending any information off your phone, we'll say so here before that version is released.
