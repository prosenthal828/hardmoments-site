Privacy Policy

Effective September 15, 2026 · Updated November 17, 2026

The short version: HardMoments doesn't collect your data. Everything you enter stays on your phone, including your account. There are no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and we never sell or share anything, because we never receive it.

What the app stores on your phone

To work, HardMoments saves what you type or choose, in the app's private storage on your device:

  • Each child's first name or nickname, and their birthday if you add it
  • The temperament sliders and the moments you chose to focus on
  • The first names of the adults on your family team
  • Each adult's private answers and the plans you sign together
  • Your "How did it go?" logs, special-time and praise logs

None of this is sent to us or to anyone else. We can't see it, and we can't recover it for you.

Your account

To use Right now, Playbook and Connect, you create an account with your name, email address and a password. That account is stored on your phone like everything else: the password is saved only as a secure hash, never as plain text, and none of it is sent to us. Because the account lives on your device, it doesn't carry over to another phone, and we can't reset your password for you.

Payments

HardMoments is free. There are no in-app purchases or subscriptions, and the app never asks for payment details.

Permissions

  • Camera and microphone. From version 1.0.27, HardMoments doesn't use or ask for the camera or microphone. Earlier versions used them only for the photo log: the camera to take a photo, and the microphone, through your phone's speech service, to dictate a note. On phones without on-device recognition, that service (usually Google's or Apple's) may have processed the audio. HardMoments never saved recordings.
  • Phone calls. When you tap a number, HardMoments hands it to your phone app. It doesn't place calls itself or read your call history.
  • Internet. Used to open the sources we cite (such as the AAP, CDC and PubMed) in an in-app browser, and to check for and download app updates. Those services have their own privacy policies.

Children

HardMoments is for adults aged 18 and older. It isn't directed at children, and we don't knowingly collect information from children. Information you enter about your children stays on your phone.

App stores

Google Play and the Apple App Store may collect information about downloads, purchases and crashes under their own privacy policies. We don't receive personal information from them about you.

Keeping it safe and deleting it

Your information is protected by your phone's own security, so we recommend using a screen lock. You can delete your account from the Family tab. Uninstalling HardMoments, or clearing its storage in your phone's settings, permanently deletes everything it saved, including your account. That includes any photos or doctor's numbers saved by versions before 1.0.27.

Not medical advice

HardMoments is a parenting app. It isn't medical or professional advice. In an emergency, call 911.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top. If HardMoments ever starts sending any information off your phone, we'll say so here before that version is released.
